Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Traina is developed and operated by WestTechLabs (WTL) (“we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use the Traina app, how we use it, and your rights. WestTechLabs is the controller of your personal data.

Contact: alice.westerbergzetterlund@gmail.com

1. Data we collect

  • Account information: email address, username and display name provided at registration, or shared by Apple or Google when you use those sign-in options.
  • Profile data: profile photo, biography, account privacy setting and app preferences you choose (such as kg or lbs and notification settings).
  • Workout data: workout templates, workout logs (exercises, sets, reps, weights), trained muscle groups, notes, durations and timestamps, and personal records calculated from them.
  • Social data: accounts you follow and follow requests, likes, comments, saved posts, training partners you tag, reports you file, accounts you block, and direct messages you send.
  • Photos and videos: media you choose to attach to workout posts.
  • Approximate location: only if you choose to tag a workout’s location. We use your device location to determine a city or area name and do not store precise GPS coordinates.
  • Push notification token: if you turn on notifications, we store your device’s push token so we can send them. It is removed when you sign out or turn notifications off.
  • Advertising data: our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects device identifiers and app usage data to serve and measure ads. See section 4.
  • Usage data: basic request logs generated by our hosting infrastructure (such as IP address and timestamps).

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your workout history and show your progress, records and streaks.
  • To show your profile, workouts and activity to you and — according to your privacy settings — to other users.
  • To provide the feed, followers, likes, comments, messages and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, and enforcing bans.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob.

We do not sell your personal data.

Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK users)

  • Contract: providing the app and its features you sign up for.
  • Consent: personalised ads and ad tracking, location tagging and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: security, preventing abuse and moderating content.

3. Who sees your data

  • Other users: each post can be Public, Followers only, or Only Me. If your account is private, only approved followers see your posts and follower lists. You can hide your weights on posts; hidden weights are not shared with other users.
  • Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf:
    • Supabase (supabase.com) — database, sign-in and file storage. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
    • Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
    • Apple and Google — if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.
    • Google AdMob — advertising (see section 4).

4. Advertising and tracking

Traina shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app usage data, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

  • In the EU/EEA and UK, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your device’s advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised based on tracking.
  • You can change these choices at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile › Settings › Advanced › Delete Account). This permanently deletes your login, profile, workouts, posts, photos and videos, comments, likes, follows and messages. You can also email us to request deletion.

6. Children

Traina is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your account and data;
  • receive your workout data in a portable format — use Profile › Settings › Export workouts (CSV), or ask us;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact alice.westerbergzetterlund@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority — in Sweden, the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

8. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections, and access to it is restricted by account-level permissions in our database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will show the new date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

10. Contact

For privacy questions: alice.westerbergzetterlund@gmail.com
