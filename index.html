Track your workouts. Share your progress.

Elja — Old Norse for endurance — is a workout log and training community for iPhone.

Log every set

Build workouts, log weights and reps in kg or lbs, and rest with the built-in timer.

See your progress

Personal-record badges, weekly streaks, charts and a calendar of every session.

Train together

Follow friends, like and comment, tag training partners and share workouts in chat.

Your privacy, your choice

Choose who sees each post, hide your weights, export or delete your data anytime.
